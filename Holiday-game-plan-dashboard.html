Paid Social converts at 0.124%. Organic Search converts at 0.476%.

Part IV The dead spend
Meta campaign ROI

Cost-per-result varies 14× across campaigns.

The Meta spend isn't broken — but the cost-per-result spread between campaigns is wide enough to warrant an attribution audit before Q4 budget locks. The standout: "DA | Sales | ATC" delivered results at $4.96 each. The Advantage Plus campaign delivered them at $70.76.

Meta Spend vs Tracked Results — Top 7 Campaigns by Spend
Orange = spend ($). Green = sales-objective results (where reported).
What this means. "DA | Sales" is the single largest campaign by spend ($12,917, 34.7% of the Meta budget) with zero recorded results — it isn't tagged with a sales objective at all. "DA | Sales | ATC" produced 291 results at $4.96 each, the cheapest meaningful spend by far. Advantage Plus delivered at 14× that cost. Pausing or auditing the untracked spend and shifting it toward the proven campaign is the actionable move before Q4 locks.
Part V What we found
Five key findings

The five things the team needs to know first.

Each finding is reproducible against breaking_games.db — see dashboard_metrics, dashboard_leaderboard, and dashboard_channels.

01 · Concentration
Two-thirds of revenue came from a single franchise — Dwellings of Eldervale at $39,813.67 (65.2%) across 10 SKUs; the base game plus its two upgrade kits alone are 50.5%. The Q4 reorder is a concentrated bet on Dwellings, not a balanced portfolio call.
02 · Untracked ad spend
"DA | Sales," the single largest Meta campaign by budget, spent $12,917.08 — 34.7% of the entire $37,200.94 Meta budget — with zero recorded results. Over a third of Q4 ad spend would otherwise renew blind.
03 · Channel conversion gap
Organic Search converts at 0.476% versus Paid Social's 0.124% — a 3.8× gap on comparable pageview volume (80,596 vs. 90,978). Q4 paid budget shouldn't scale with Paid Social's traffic volume alone.
04 · Retention gap
Only 12 of 1,021 unique customers in the bot-filtered checkout data came back for a second cart — a 1.2% repeat rate. The biggest untapped lever in the business isn't cart abandonment; it's a retention loop that doesn't exist yet.
05 · Abandonment, corrected
The raw cart abandonment rate (all-time carts against one month of orders) reads as 91.9%. Filter bot carts and align the window to July 2025 and the real rate is 50.8% — inside normal e-commerce range, not a crisis that justifies a checkout rebuild.
